> A stigma is a supernatural ability granted to someone who successfully "devours a demon" and becomes a ghoul.

> Each stigma is unique and, in most cases, invoked through an incantation related to their Ars Goetic demon. Most ghouls must speak it aloud, though some can invoke it mentally.

> Cornelius had said that Jin Kamurai once did not require an incantation to activate his stigma. Hyde believes this correlates with either proficiency or mental state.

> Stigmas can manifest passively, as I've learned through my encounter with Jiro Kirisaki, where I (somewhat in response to past trauma) grabbed his arms and dug my nails in his wrist for shoving a patient gown at me, asking I change into it without explaining why, and the pain was simultaneously dealt back to me.
