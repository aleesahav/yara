Brice Du Verre is the second son of patriarch [Gaultier Du Verre](#profiles:gaultier), the younger brother of [Armond Du Verre](#profiles:armond), and the husband of [Pia Santana](#profiles:pia). He worked as genetic researcher, for [The Department of Genetic Research](#departmetns:dagr) throughout his entire career, eventually specializing study in sentient artifacts and theorized that artifacts were more creatures than magical items.  

Brice is treated like the black sheep of his family. He left both his family and his status as a French nobleman to relocate to the United States with his share of Gaultier’s fortune, during the time when his father fell ill. Upon arriving in the West, he constructed a private mansion in the Marin Headlands and accepted a position within Aether Academy as the Director of Special Operations, specifically overseeing artifacts that end up in civilian hands. 

Brice claimed he left because he knew he “wasn’t going to be enough” for Gaultier. Classic second-son syndrome, but his timing with leaving France was odd, having left when [Luciana](#profiles:luciana), his niece, fell ill and required long term hospitalization in Japan's Darkwick Academy.<!-- pagebreak -->

Not long after, he met Pia Santana—an ordinary woman—and married her. Supposedly, wanting to shield her from the anomalous world, Brice stepped back from direct Institute involvement and now works only as a consultant to Aether Academy when artifacts or some unclassifiable (genetically strange) anomalies need a second pair of eyes. He often spends little time with work and has high-risk artifacts or intel on odd anomalies sent to Darkwick Academy for further study.  

He is my adoptive father.
