**Born:** September 22, 1994  
**AII Alias:** “Sun”  
**Current Role:** Professor of Anomalous Combat, Aether West

Luciana Yoon Du Verre is the only daughter of [Armond Du Verre](#profiles:armond), President of the French AII branch, [Sidereal Academy](#branches:sidereal). That makes her my adopted cousin.<!-- pagebreak -->

Everything I know about Luciana comes either from her own careful words or from letters I snooped on at the Du Verre mansion. She’s a deeply private person—every sentence measured, every silence deliberate.

She was homeschooled by her mother, [Soojin](#profiles:soojin), from a young age and raised between France and Japan. When she was eight years old, she became critically ill, resulting in the loss of both arms. Because her illness occurred in Japan, she remained there even after the acute phase passed, undergoing long-term rehabilitation and integration with specialized bilateral artifact prosthetics.

Due to spending much of her childhood in clinical environments, her education remained private. Despite this, she excelled academically and was accepted into Sidereal Academy at eighteen—despite it being a post-baccalaureate institution.<!-- pagebreak -->

I learned most of this through letters she sent to [Brice](#profiles:brice), her uncle—my adoptive father—whom she seemed particularly close to. It’s also why I suspect she accepted a faculty position at Aether Academy, and why she chose to move into his home when I turned eighteen.

Luciana doesn’t like talking about herself. She *does*, however, ask an alarming number of questions—strategic ones, like she’s testing you. I never had a reason to lie to her… except once, about snooping around the Du Verre mansion. She asked again afterward, clearly expecting the truth.

So I gave it to her.  
I haven’t lied to her since.

### Additional Notes

- She is known to the family as "Luce" but she hates being called that name. She said once that it had more to do with the fact that her father wanted her to be a boy, and calling her "Luce" was his attempt at changing her gender. She claimed she didn't care whether she was a boy or a girl, it was more so that her father wanted her to be something that she wasn't. <!-- pagebreak -->
- Her bilateral prosthetic arms are called [Aeon Arms](#artifacts:aeonarms).
- In addition, she possesses a licensed artifact known as [Threshold](#artifacts:threshold), which are brass knuckles.
- She exhibits exceptional control over her bodily control. She's exceptionally athletic, and seems to be light on her feet, always appearing without me ever hearing her footsteps. I can usually detect the slightest footstep, a mouse scurrying, but never hers. 
- She's incredibly hard to hit, nearly impossible to disarm, or throw off balance. She's the best hand to hand fighter I know.
