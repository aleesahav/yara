**Threshold Knuckles** is the personal artifact of <a href="#profiles:luciana">Luciana Yoon Du Verre</a>.

The artifact takes the form of lightweight brass knuckles that is capable of briefly changing the structure of the target. For solid structures, a strike can turning any surface into something she can pass through without breaking it. When using it against something with a heartbeat, it's power does not activate.
